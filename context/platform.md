# NexPortal — platform context (stated assumptions)

The gate judges hidden dependencies against this file, not against vibes. Every line is an assumption about NexPortal made for the prototype; correct the file and the gate's judgement moves with it.

- **Learning happens in Canvas.** myNXU is the shell; every course card opens Canvas via LTI. Coursework, module items, submissions and grades live in Canvas. Whether item-level module completion is readable from myNXU is unknown.
- **Courses are 8 weeks**, weekly modules, assignments due Friday. At most two courses plus a seminar per term; the second course is gated on CGPA ≥ 3.00; adds close on day 5 of the term; course selection opens on a dated window before the term.
- **Monthly starts, pay-as-you-go tuition.** Invoices are issued per term instalment through an external invoicing/payments provider. Whether the provider supports paying a subset of open invoices, or a partial amount against one, is unknown.
- **Government-ID verification** runs through an external vendor; a document upload carries a deadline on the rail.
- **Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP):** good standing / warning / probation / eligible for dismissal, judged on CGPA (minimum 2.00), pace (≥ 67% of attempted credits earned) and maximum time frame (150% of programme length). Standing is the weakest of the three.
- **Referral programme:** a learner shares a code or a link; when the referred learner starts, both are credited toward tuition in the wallet. Eligibility depends on the learner's market; markets and amounts are set by finance.
- **Credentials stack:** coursework counts toward certificates inside the degree; a learner cannot enrol in a second programme while one is active.
- **The support assistant "Nexa"** answers programme, payment and deadline questions from the catalogue and the learner's own record.
- **The team:** a small engineering team, one designer, no dedicated PM until now. Board states: Inbox → Triaged → Drafted → Ready → In Sprint → Done. Triage is Thursday; a sprint is two weeks.
- **Events available to myNXU today:** assignment due (from Canvas), grade posted, credential earned, invoice issued/paid, document status changed, registration window opened — they drive the deadline rail.
