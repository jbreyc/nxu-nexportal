# NexPortal — platform context (stated assumptions)

The gate judges hidden dependencies against this file, not against vibes. Every line is an assumption about NexPortal made for the prototype, and each one is leaned on by a fixture or a spec on the board; correct the file and the gate's judgement moves with it.

- **Learning happens in Canvas.** myNXU is the shell; every course card opens Canvas via LTI. Coursework, submissions and grades live in Canvas. Whether item-level module completion is readable from myNXU is unknown.
- **The term calendar.** Courses are 8 weeks, weekly modules, assignments due Friday. At most two courses plus a seminar per term; the second course is gated on CGPA ≥ 3.00; adds close on day 5 of the term; course selection opens on a dated window before the term.
- **Tuition and invoices.** Monthly starts, pay-as-you-go. Invoices are issued per term instalment through an external invoicing/payments provider. Whether the provider supports paying a subset of open invoices, or a partial amount against one, is unknown.
- **Government-ID verification** runs through an external vendor; a document request carries a deadline.
- **The referral programme.** A learner shares a code or a link; when the referred learner starts, both are credited toward tuition in the wallet. Eligibility and amounts depend on the learner's market and are set by finance.
- **The support assistant "Nexa"** answers programme, payment and deadline questions from the catalogue and the learner's own record.
- **The team.** A small engineering team, one designer, no dedicated PM until now. Board states: Inbox → Triaged → Drafted → Ready → In Sprint → Done. Triage is Thursday; a sprint is two weeks.
- **Events available to myNXU today:** assignment due (from Canvas), grade posted, credential earned, invoice issued/paid, document status changed, registration window opened — they drive the deadline rail.
